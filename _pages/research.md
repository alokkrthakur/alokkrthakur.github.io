# Research

My research combines **experimental measurements, aerosol science, computational modeling, atmospheric modeling, and machine learning** to understand air pollution across indoor and outdoor environments.

## Research Areas

### 🌫️ Indoor Air Quality

Investigating indoor particle dynamics, source contributions, exposure, sensing, transport, and mitigation strategies.

### 🧪 Aerosol Science

Aerosol measurement, chemical characterization, source identification, and atmospheric implications across laboratory, indoor, and outdoor environments.

### 🌎 Atmospheric Modeling

Using atmospheric and transport modeling approaches to understand pollutant dispersion, transformation, source impacts, and air-quality patterns.

### 🤖 Machine Learning

Applying machine learning and physics-informed data-driven approaches to prediction, source characterization, and understanding complex air-quality processes.

### 💨 Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)

Using computational fluid dynamics, including OpenFOAM, to study airflow, pollutant transport, and particle dynamics in built and environmental systems.

## Methods & Tools

**Experimental:** aerosol instrumentation · PM₂.₅ measurements · chemical analysis · chamber studies · sensor development

**Modeling:** computational fluid dynamics · OpenFOAM · atmospheric and transport modeling · emission inventories

**Data science:** machine learning · physics-informed learning · statistical analysis · visualization
