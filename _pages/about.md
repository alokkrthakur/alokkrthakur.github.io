# About Me

I am a researcher working at the intersection of **aerosol science, air quality, environmental measurements, computational modeling, and emerging data-driven methods**.

I am currently a **Postdoctoral Researcher at Colorado State University**, where my research focuses on particulate emissions from wildland–urban interface fires and the combustion of structural materials.

## Research Background

My research experience spans:

- Wildland–urban interface fire emissions
- Structural-material combustion and PM₂.₅ characterization
- Indoor air quality and aerosol exposure
- Low-cost air-quality sensing
- Aerosol measurement and chemical characterization
- Computational fluid dynamics using OpenFOAM
- Physics-informed machine learning

## Research Philosophy

My goal is to connect laboratory measurements, field observations, computational models, and data-driven approaches to better understand air-pollution sources and their impacts on the built and natural environment.
