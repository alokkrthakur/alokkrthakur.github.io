This paper reviews the current measurement, modeling, and mitigation status of indoor air pollution in India, along with a comparative discussion of similar work conducted globally. It identifies critical knowledge gaps in measurements, source characterization, transport, exposure assessment, modeling, and mitigation in urban-Indian built environments.
