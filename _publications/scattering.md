This study evaluates a low-cost PM sensor (PurpleAir) against reference instruments during cooking experiments in a test house. The results show strong agreement for PM1, PM2.5, and PM10 under many conditions, while sensor response depends on particle size distribution and optical properties. Sub-300 nm particles contributed substantially to total light-scattering intensity, highlighting limitations associated with size classification and sensor calibration.
