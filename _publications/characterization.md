This study uses spatio-temporal monitoring in a multizonal residential apartment to characterize particulate matter emission sources, transport, spatial exposure, indoor-to-outdoor ratios, and mitigation strategies. Cooking and incense generated substantial sub-micron particle concentrations. Natural ventilation through balcony doors reduced average exposure by 74–86% across zones, while air-conditioning and portable air-cleaning strategies provided additional reductions.
