# Research

My research combines **experimental measurements, aerosol chemistry, computational modeling, and machine learning** to understand air pollution across the built and natural environment.

## Research Areas

### 🔥 Wildland–Urban Interface Fires

Understanding particulate emissions from fires that involve vegetation and built structures, with emphasis on PM₂.₅ mass, chemical composition, source characteristics, and emission factors.

[WUI Fires →](/research/wui-fires/)

### 🏠 Structural Materials

Characterizing emissions from residential construction materials and mixed-fuel assemblies under different combustion conditions and experimental scales.

[Structural Materials →](/research/structural-materials/)

### 🌫️ Indoor Air Quality

Investigating indoor particle dynamics, source contributions, exposure, sensing, transport, and mitigation strategies.

[Indoor Air Quality →](/research/indoor-air-quality/)

### 🧪 Aerosol Science

Aerosol measurement, chemical characterization, source identification, and atmospheric implications across laboratory, indoor, and outdoor environments.

[Aerosol Science →](/research/aerosol-science/)

## Methods & Tools

**Experimental:** aerosol instrumentation · PM₂.₅ measurements · chemical analysis · chamber studies · sensor development

**Modeling:** computational fluid dynamics · OpenFOAM · transport modeling · emission inventories

**Data science:** machine learning · physics-informed learning · statistical analysis · visualization
