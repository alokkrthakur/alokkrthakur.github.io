# Structural Materials

Residential buildings contain a complex mixture of wood products, plastics, insulation, textiles, finishes, and other materials. Their combustion can produce substantially different aerosol emissions from those of untreated biomass.

## Research Focus

- PM₂.₅ emission factors from structural materials
- Elemental and organic aerosol composition
- Flaming and pyrolysis conditions
- Material-specific source profiles
- Mixed-fuel structural assemblies
- Scaling laboratory measurements toward realistic fire conditions
