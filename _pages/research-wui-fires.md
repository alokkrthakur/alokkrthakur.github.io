# Wildland–Urban Interface Fires

Wildland–urban interface (WUI) fires can involve the combustion of both vegetation and built structures. My current research focuses on understanding how structural materials contribute to particulate emissions during these events.

## Key Questions

- How do structural materials change PM₂.₅ emissions compared with biomass fuels?
- How does combustion condition affect aerosol composition?
- Which chemical species can help identify structural-material sources?
- How can laboratory emission factors be translated to real WUI fire events?
