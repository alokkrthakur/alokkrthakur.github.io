# Aerosol Science

My broader research interests in aerosol science include particle measurement, chemical characterization, source identification, exposure, and the environmental and health implications of particulate matter.

## Research Themes

- Aerosol measurement and instrumentation
- PM₂.₅ and ultrafine particles
- Chemical composition of particulate matter
- Source profiles and emission factors
- Aerosol exposure and toxicity
- Atmospheric and indoor aerosol processes
